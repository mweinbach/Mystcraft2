# EMC values for `icbm_port`

Generated by `python3 scripts/emc.py generate` from `policy.json` and `registry.txt`; do not edit. See [docs/emc.md](../../../docs/emc.md) for the method and the ProjectE/Item Alchemy loader notes.

142 registered items: 3 explicit, 139 recipe. `recipe` values are derived from crafting, smelting, stonecutting and smithing recipes, `tag` values come from the EMC mod's own tag defaults, and `explicit` values are listed below. Full per-item values are in `values.json`.

The nuclear, antimatter and red matter tiers are valued at the cheapest of their recipes, derived like every other explosive: crafting and burning one never gains EMC under any recipe, and transmuting one costs as much as its cheapest ingredients.

Their atomic-alloy recipes load only with Mekanism, and the uranium nuclear recipe only when a mod (Mekanism among them) fills the uranium tags. Item Alchemy uses the shipped values rather than recalculating them, so emc.py prices those alternatives with Mekanism's own shipped values (atomic alloy, uranium ingot) and takes the minimum over every recipe, including the alloy recipes a populated uranium tag switches off. Without Mekanism these tiers are therefore worth less than their nether-star and skull recipes cost to craft, which never gains EMC. A uranium provider other than Mekanism is not priced; ProjectE's live mapper still uses the cheaper recipe, and Item Alchemy stays loop-free unless the pack values that uranium below the alloy path.

The carrier, held-item and cluster loading recipes only move one cargo item into NBT and back, or turn bomblets into a launch manifest, so they create nothing; transmuting a loaded carrier or missile gives an empty one.
