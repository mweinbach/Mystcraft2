# EMC values for `buildcraft`

Generated by `python3 scripts/emc.py generate` from `policy.json` and `registry.txt`; do not edit. See [docs/emc.md](../../../docs/emc.md) for the method and the ProjectE/Item Alchemy loader notes.

79 registered items: 1 conversion, 33 excluded, 1 explicit, 44 recipe. `recipe` values are derived from crafting, smelting, stonecutting and smithing recipes, `tag` values come from the EMC mod's own tag defaults, and `explicit` values are listed below. Full per-item values are in `values.json`.

Every survival BuildCraft item in this port apart from fluid buckets is crafted, so its value comes from its recipe: gears, the wrench, engines, machines (including the distiller and heat exchanger), the tank, land and path marks, the marker connector, waterproof and all pipes (including the shapeless recipes that turn fluid and kinesis pipes back into item pipes).

The mining well, quarry and pump take blocks and fluids from the world for power alone, as a player with a pickaxe or bucket would; they consume no items, so there is no item process to check for EMC gain.

Oil generates in wells and regrows above large wells' springs, so it is a base fluid valued like lava. Every refinery route to light fuel turns a bucket of oil into half a bucket of light fuel: crude oil distils into heavy oil, and hot oil into mixed light fuels, each of which yields light fuel after heating or cooling. Light fuel is therefore worth the two buckets of oil it takes, ignoring the lava and water that heat and cool it, so no route can make it for less. The other refinery fluids are by-products or intermediate heats of that chain and stay unvalued: each distillation turns one fluid into more volume of two others, so valuing those as well would let a split create EMC.
