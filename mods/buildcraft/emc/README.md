# EMC values for `buildcraft`

Generated by `python3 scripts/emc.py generate` from `policy.json` and `registry.txt`; do not edit. See [docs/emc.md](../../../docs/emc.md) for the method and the ProjectE/Item Alchemy loader notes.

43 registered items: 4 excluded, 39 recipe. `recipe` values are derived from crafting, smelting, stonecutting and smithing recipes, `tag` values come from the EMC mod's own tag defaults, and `explicit` values are listed below. Full per-item values are in `values.json`.

Every survival BuildCraft item in this port is crafted, so its value comes from its recipe: gears, the wrench, engines, machines, the tank, waterproof and all pipes (including the shapeless recipes that turn fluid and kinesis pipes back into item pipes).

The mining well, quarry and pump take blocks and fluids from the world for power alone, as a player with a pickaxe or bucket would; they consume no items, so there is no item process to check for EMC gain. Oil worldgen, the refinery and the distiller are not ported, which is why the oil-family buckets have no survival source.
