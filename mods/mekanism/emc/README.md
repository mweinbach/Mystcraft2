# EMC values for `mekanism`

This policy also covers `mekanismgenerators`, `mekanismtools`, `mekanismadditions`. Each namespace's shipped files go to that namespace's output root.

Generated by `python3 scripts/emc.py generate` from `policy.json` and `registry.txt`; do not edit. See [docs/emc.md](../../../docs/emc.md) for the method and the ProjectE/Item Alchemy loader notes.

779 registered items: 267 conversion, 25 excluded, 12 explicit, 456 recipe, 19 tag. `recipe` values are derived from crafting, smelting, stonecutting and smithing recipes, `tag` values come from the EMC mod's own tag defaults, and `explicit` values are listed below. Full per-item values are in `values.json`.

Survival items are priced at their crafting recipe, or at the item cost of the machine recipe when that is the way to make them. Energy stored on an item is not part of the cost: transmutation does not copy that data.

Osmium ingots cost one and a half iron ingots. The osmium rail recipe yields twenty-four rails from six ingots, and a cheaper osmium ingot would make vanilla rails cheaper to transmute than to craft. Osmium nuggets are excluded because 384 does not divide by nine.

Uranium ingots follow ProjectE's uranium ingot tag (4096). Raw uranium matches the ingot, and uranium nuggets are excluded because 4096 does not divide by nine.

Tin and lead ores and raw ores are fixed at Item Alchemy's tag values. Tin and lead nuggets then settle slightly under the raw ore; the ingot is not fixed, so that split does not fight a tag.
Hydrogen, oxygen, chlorine, brine, lithium, sodium, superheated sodium, steam, ethene, heavy water and hydrogen chloride are made from water, so a bucket of each costs an empty bucket. Nutritional paste is not: a bucket is twenty food points of dried kelp plus the bucket. Sulfur dioxide, sulfur trioxide and sulfuric acid are ten sulfur dust per bucket, because the infuser preserves that volume; hydrofluoric acid, uranium oxide and uranium hexafluoride are priced from the items that produce them.
Sawdust is fixed at 64 because three sawdust craft six paper. The sawmill's plank and log recipes turn vanilla logs into more planks than crafting does; both ends of that recipe are vanilla, so it is not a conversion.

Sulfur dust matches gunpowder, because injecting gunpowder and enriching the dust are one-to-one. Coal gasification also makes sulfur from coal, which is worth less than gunpowder; that recipe is not a conversion, or sulfur could not match gunpowder.

Ore factories that double raw ore or silk-touched ore are not conversions. Crystals, shards, clumps and dirty dust are worth one dust, and one dust is one ingot.

Hydrogen, oxygen, chlorine, brine, lithium, sodium, superheated sodium, steam, ethene, heavy water, nutritional paste and hydrogen chloride are made from water. A bucket of each costs an empty bucket. Sulfur dioxide, sulfur trioxide, sulfuric acid, hydrofluoric acid, uranium oxide and uranium hexafluoride are priced from the items that produce them.

One substrate and ethene make eight substrate. That reaction is not a conversion, or substrate would be worth a fraction of itself. Substrate is priced at two bio fuel, which crushing plants produces.

Polonium and plutonium pellets are priced at the fluorite dust their reactions consume. The fission reactor's fuel cycle is not an EMC conversion yet. One antimatter pellet is a thousand polonium pellets, which is how many millibuckets of polonium the SPS spends.

Lithium dust, salt and salt blocks are evaporated from water and are excluded. Induction cells and providers are priced without that dust. ProjectE 1.1.0 does not value an elytra; the HDPE elytra uses Item Alchemy's elytra value (16463) on Forge so the craft resolves there too.

Chemical bucket processes check the volume ratios. A route that fills a bucket for less than its value fails the check, which is what the old sulfuric-acid price (one dust per 2 mB, five hundred per bucket) and a paste bucket priced as an empty bucket both did.

Generators items with a shaped crafting recipe take that recipe's cost. The wind generator is stored as mek_data and the hohlraum is a metallurgic infusion, so each has a conversion at its item cost, the same way the core module prices those recipe types. Bioethanol, deuterium, tritium and fusion-fuel buckets are filled by generator processes and have no item recipe, so they are excluded until those processes are priced.

Tools and armor take their crafting cost. A paxel recipe is a shaped craft of an axe, a pickaxe, a shovel and two sticks, and smithing turns a diamond paxel into a netherite paxel. The banner shield recipe adds a banner to an existing shield and creates no item. Smelting or blasting a tool returns one nugget, which is worth less than the tool.

Plastic blocks, transparent plastic and glow panels ignore the dye in their cost. Dark red and aqua have no dye recipe, and the painting machine recolors every plastic family, so a cheaper dye must not make a more expensive color. Slick plastic follows the enrichment chamber, one plastic block to one slick block, which is cheaper than the slimeball craft. Slabs come out below one EMC and ship as 0.
