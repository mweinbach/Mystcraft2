# EMC values for `advancedgenetics`

Generated by `python3 scripts/emc.py generate` from `policy.json` and `registry.txt`; do not edit. See [docs/emc.md](../../../docs/emc.md) for the method and the ProjectE/Item Alchemy loader notes.

94 registered items: 40 conversion, 39 explicit, 15 recipe. `recipe` values are derived from crafting, smelting, stonecutting and smithing recipes, `tag` values come from the EMC mod's own tag defaults, and `explicit` values are listed below. Full per-item values are in `values.json`.

Genes such as Emerald Heart, Lay Egg, Milky, Wooly and Meaty make vanilla items renewable on a cooldown; that is gene balance, not an EMC value, and is left to the pack's gene configuration.
